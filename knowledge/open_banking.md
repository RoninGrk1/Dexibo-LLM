# Open Banking

In the UK and EU, regulated frameworks (including PSD2 and Open Banking standards) let customers grant trusted third parties secure access to account data or payment initiation via APIs — instead of sharing bank passwords.

## Roles
- **AIS** — Account Information Services (read balances/transactions with consent)
- **PIS** — Payment Initiation Services (start payments with consent)
- **ASPSP** — Account-holding banks/building societies

## Safeguards
- Providers must be authorised by regulators (e.g. FCA in the UK)
- Consent is time-bounded and can be revoked
- Strong customer authentication (SCA) applies in many flows
