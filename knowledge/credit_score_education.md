# Credit scores (educational)

A **credit score** (or credit file) is a lender’s shorthand for how you have used credit in the past. Higher scores generally mean easier access to loans and cards — but formulas differ by bureau and lender.

## What typically influences scores
- Payment history (on-time vs missed payments).
- Credit utilisation (balances relative to limits).
- Length of credit history.
- Recent applications / hard searches.
- Mix of credit types (in some models).

## Healthy habits (general education)
- Pay at least the minimum on time, every time.
- Avoid maxing out cards; leaving headroom can help utilisation metrics.
- Check your file for errors and report mistakes to the bureau.
- Don’t close old accounts casually — length of history can matter.

## What a score is not
- Not a character judgement.
- Not the only factor lenders use (income, affordability, and policy matter).
- Not identical across Experian, Equifax, TransUnion, or open-banking affordability checks.

Educational only — not credit advice. Check your local bureau’s guidance.
