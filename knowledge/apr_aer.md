# APR and AER

## APR (Annual Percentage Rate)
A standardised yearly cost of **borrowing**, including interest and certain mandatory fees. Helps compare credit cards, loans, and mortgages — but may not capture every optional charge or your personal usage pattern.

## AER (Annual Equivalent Rate)
A standardised yearly rate for **savings**, showing what you would earn if interest were compounded and paid once a year. Useful when comparing accounts with different compounding frequencies.

## Tip
APR is for debt products; AER is for savings. Do not mix them when comparing.

Illustrative education only — product terms vary by lender and jurisdiction.
