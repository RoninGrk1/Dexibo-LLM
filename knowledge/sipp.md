# SIPP (Self-Invested Personal Pension)

A UK personal pension where the holder chooses investments within HMRC and scheme rules. Popular with people who want more control than a default workplace fund.

## Basics
- Contributions may attract tax relief (subject to annual and lifetime-related limits).
- Investments can include funds, ETFs, shares, bonds, and sometimes commercial property (provider-dependent).
- Access is generally from the normal minimum pension age (rising over time) with tax implications on withdrawals.
