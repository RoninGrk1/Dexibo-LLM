# Volatility and Sharpe ratio (educational)

## Volatility
A statistical measure of how much returns vary around their average. Higher volatility means larger swings — up and down. Common input to risk models, but it does not capture every risk (liquidity, credit default, tail events).

## Sharpe ratio (concept)
Excess return over a risk-free rate, divided by the standard deviation of returns. Often read as “return per unit of volatility.” Assumptions and the sample period matter a lot.
