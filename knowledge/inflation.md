# Inflation and purchasing power

Inflation is a sustained rise in the general price level, which erodes the purchasing power of cash. Central banks often target a low positive inflation rate.

## UK measures
- **CPI** and related indices are published by the ONS
- Real returns adjust nominal returns for inflation

## Why it matters for savers
Cash that earns less than inflation loses real value over time. That is a general educational point — not a product recommendation.
