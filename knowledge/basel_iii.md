# Basel III (overview)

Basel III is an international banking regulatory framework from the Basel Committee on Banking Supervision. It strengthens capital, leverage, and liquidity standards after the global financial crisis.

## High-level pillars
- Higher and better-quality **capital** requirements
- **Leverage ratio** as a non-risk-based backstop
- **Liquidity** metrics such as LCR (Liquidity Coverage Ratio) and NSFR (Net Stable Funding Ratio)
- Risk coverage enhancements (credit, market, operational)

## Context
National supervisors implement Basel standards into local rules. Details are complex and evolve. This is a conceptual overview for education — not a compliance manual.
