# Bonds and UK gilts

A bond is a debt instrument: the issuer borrows money, typically pays interest (coupon), and repays principal at maturity.

## UK angle
- UK government bonds are called **gilts**
- Corporate bonds carry credit risk of the issuer
- Bond prices and yields move inversely; interest-rate risk matters

## Risks
- Interest-rate risk, credit risk, inflation risk, liquidity risk

Educational concept only — not a recommendation to buy or sell any bond.
