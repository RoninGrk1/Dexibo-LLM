# FX spread (currency conversion)

When you exchange currency, providers rarely use the mid-market rate you see on Google. The **FX spread** is the gap between the buy and sell rates — a common way services earn money on conversions.

## Key ideas
- **Mid-market rate**: midpoint between buy and sell quotes on wholesale markets.
- **Spread**: difference between the rate you get and the mid-market (or between bid/ask).
- **Fees**: some providers add an explicit fee *plus* a spread; others advertise “zero fees” but widen the spread.

## Comparing providers
- Look at the **all-in cost**: effective rate after spread and fees.
- Watch weekend/holiday markups and tourist-bureau cash rates.
- Card overseas spending may use the network rate plus a foreign-transaction fee.

## Example framing
If mid-market GBP/EUR is 1.1600 and you receive 1.1400, the difference is the spread (before any flat fee).
