# ETF (Exchange-Traded Fund)

An investment fund that typically tracks an index, sector, or strategy and trades on an exchange like a share.

## Why people use them
- Diversified exposure in one instrument
- Often lower ongoing charges than active funds (not always)
- Intraday liquidity during market hours

## Risks and costs
- Market risk of the underlying assets
- Tracking difference vs the index
- Bid–ask spreads and dealing costs
- Currency risk for overseas holdings
- Not all ETFs are “low risk” (e.g. leveraged or thematic products)

Educational concept — not a recommendation to buy any ETF.
