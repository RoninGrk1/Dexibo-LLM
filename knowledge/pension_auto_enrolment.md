# UK pension auto-enrolment

**Auto-enrolment** requires eligible UK employers to enrol qualifying staff into a workplace pension and pay a minimum contribution. Workers can opt out, but the policy aim is to boost retirement saving.

## Eligibility (high level)
- Age and earnings thresholds apply (check current GOV.UK figures).
- Employers choose a qualifying scheme (often NEST or a commercial provider).

## Contributions
- Minimum total contributions are set in law and shared between employer and worker (plus tax relief in many cases).
- Figures and bands change over time — always verify current rates.

## Why it matters
- Compounding over decades can materially change retirement outcomes.
- Opting out reduces long-term pot size; re-enrolment cycles may bring people back in.
- Workplace pensions are distinct from personal SIPPs and the State Pension.
