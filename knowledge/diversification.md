# Diversification and asset allocation

Diversification means spreading investments across assets, sectors, or geographies so one bad event hurts the whole portfolio less. It reduces idiosyncratic risk but does **not** eliminate market risk.

## Related ideas
- **Asset allocation** — mix of equities, bonds, cash, alternatives
- **Correlation** — how assets move together (can rise in crises)
- **Rebalancing** — restoring target weights over time

Allocation should match goals, time horizon, and risk tolerance. Seek regulated advice for personal situations. Educational only.
