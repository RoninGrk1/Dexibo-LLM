# SEPA (Single Euro Payments Area)

SEPA harmonises euro payments across participating European countries so euro transfers can work more like domestic payments.

## Common instruments
- **SEPA Credit Transfer (SCT)** — push payments in euro
- **SEPA Instant Credit Transfer** — near-real-time where available
- **SEPA Direct Debit** — pull payments with a mandate

## Notes
- Uses IBANs; BIC may still appear in some flows
- Fees and cut-off times depend on the bank/PSP
- Non-euro currencies are outside classic SEPA euro rails
