# ISA (Individual Savings Account)

A UK tax-advantaged wrapper for cash or investments. Contributions count toward an annual allowance set by HMRC. Returns inside an ISA are generally free of UK income tax and capital gains tax on investments held.

## Types
- **Cash ISA** — deposit-style savings with tax-free interest.
- **Stocks & Shares ISA** — holds funds, ETFs, shares, and similar investments.
- **Lifetime ISA** — for first-time buyers / house deposit goals (rules apply).
- **Innovative Finance ISA** — peer-to-peer / crowdfunding style products.

## Key points
- Annual subscription allowance is set each tax year (check GOV.UK).
- You can usually pay into one of each type per tax year under current flexibilities — rules evolve.
- Transfers between providers are common; use official ISA transfer processes.
