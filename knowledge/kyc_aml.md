# KYC and AML

## KYC (Know Your Customer)
Processes firms use to verify identity and assess risk before opening accounts or providing certain services. Related: CDD (Customer Due Diligence), enhanced due diligence for higher-risk cases.

## AML (Anti-Money Laundering)
Laws and controls that aim to stop criminals disguising illicit funds as legitimate. Typical obligations:
- Customer due diligence
- Transaction monitoring
- Suspicious activity reporting
- Record keeping

## Important
Circumventing KYC/AML controls is illegal. Dexibo will not help with evasion, structuring, or fraud. Legitimate questions about *why* checks exist and what documents firms usually request are fine at a high level.
